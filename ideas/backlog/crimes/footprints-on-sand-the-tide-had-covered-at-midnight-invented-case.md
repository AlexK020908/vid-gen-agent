## Where it's known from

NOAA National Ocean Service, 'How frequent are tides?': 'Coastal areas experience two low tides and two high tides every lunar day, or 24 hours and 50 minutes' (a few coasts have one a day). The times are predicted ahead and published, as NOAA's Tides and Currents service does. That the rising sea smooths footprints out of wet sand, that seaweed is left along the high-water line, and that a print's toe shows which way someone walked are my own steps: everyday sights on a beach, not NOAA's words.
