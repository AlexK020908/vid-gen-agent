## Where it's known from

Met Office, 'What is frost and ice?': at night objects 'lose energy back into space', hoar frost is vapour turning straight to ice 'on grass and cars', and cloud acts 'like a blanket' to stop surfaces 'losing heat so quickly'. It also says 'The grass tends to lose more heat than tarmac', so tarmac can stay clear while cars and grass frost, which is why the lane is grass. That a parked car shields the ground beneath it the way cloud does, that frost which formed while his car was away is still under it at dawn except where the warm engine melted it, that a warm engine keeps its bonnet clear, and that tyres leave dark lines in frost are my own steps from that: things anyone can see on a frosty morning, not the Met Office's words.
