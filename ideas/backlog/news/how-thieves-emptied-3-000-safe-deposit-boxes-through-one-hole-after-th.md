# How thieves emptied 3,000 safe deposit boxes through one hole, after the alarm had rung (Dec 2025)

## The first second

**Heard:** Thieves emptied three thousand safe deposit boxes through one hole in a wall.

**Seen:** A thick concrete wall with one clean round hole bored through it, and through the hole a strongroom lined floor to ceiling with small steel doors, every one hanging open.

## Why someone would stop

- **With the sound off:** One neat round hole in a concrete wall, and behind it thousands of little locked doors all standing open. A viewer sees the whole crime in one frame and wants to know how nobody stopped it.
- **What's at stake:** About 2,700 customers' savings, gold and family jewellery, kept in the one place they thought was safest, and insured on average for only about 10,000 euros a box.

## How it goes

**Area:** a heist that isn't crypto

**Happened:** Over the weekend after Christmas 2025, thieves got from a multi-storey car park into the basement of a Sparkasse bank in Gelsenkirchen, Germany, drilled through the vault wall and broke open more than 95 percent of its 3,250 safe deposit boxes. A fire alarm from the vault had rung on the Saturday, been checked and logged as false, and the break-in was found only when another alarm rang on the Monday.

**When:** 27 to 29 December 2025; found at 03:58 on Monday 29 December. Police statements from 30 December 2025; the state interior minister's account to the state parliament was reported by February 2026. Nobody has been arrested as far as I could find (latest report I found: March 2026), so who did it is not settled, and the short says so.

**Newcomer:** A safe deposit box is a small locker you rent inside the bank's strongroom: you hold the key, and the bank doesn't know what's in it. A vault is a room with concrete walls and one heavy door, so the wall is the part nobody watches. A core drill is the hollow drill builders use to cut a round plug out of concrete for a pipe. A roll shutter is the metal blind pulled down over the way to the vault when the bank is shut. Insured value is the most the bank's cover pays for a box, whatever was inside.

**Lesson:** The alarm worked and was answered, and nobody could see past a shutter. Nobody has been caught, so nobody knows who did it. The habit it teaches is a dull one: a box is insured only up to a limit, about 10,000 euros here, unless you insure what's inside yourself.

**Said by:** Nobody has been named or charged, and the short says nobody knows who did it. Gelsenkirchen police, through a spokesman to AFP, said the break-in was 'indeed very professionally executed'; the bank said the boxes were broken into 'by unknown perpetrators'; the police chief called it 'one of the biggest criminal cases in the history of the state of North Rhine-Westphalia'; the state's interior minister told a state parliament committee the Saturday responders saw 'no smoke, no smell of fire, or damage' and 'concluded it was a false alarm'. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/31/germany-hunts-christmas-thieves-after-oceans-eleven-style-bank-heist . Reports say most victims were of Turkish or Arab origin; the short leaves that out and names no customer.

1. An escape door between the car park and the bank is tampered with so it no longer shuts. Police say that gave the thieves a free way in from the car park.
2. They get past several security systems to an archive room in the basement, a room of old files that shares a wall with the vault.
3. A large drill bores a round hole straight through the thick concrete, and the vault's heavy door is never touched.
4. Saturday 27 December: a fire alarm rings from the vault. Firefighters and police come. The way to the vault is behind a locked shutter, they find 'no smoke, no smell of fire, or damage', it goes down as a false alarm, and they leave.
5. The bank's own computers log the first box forced at 10:45 that day and the last one logged at 14:44. That night witnesses see men carrying big bags down the car park stairs.
6. Monday 29 December, 03:58: another alarm. This time responders find the hole, and nearly every box open. Cameras show a black car leaving the car park with masked people inside, on plates from a car stolen 200 km away.

**The ending:** The round hole in the wall with police tape across it, and one small steel door swinging shut on an empty box. Said: 'The alarm rang on Saturday. They found the hole on Monday. Nobody has been caught.'
