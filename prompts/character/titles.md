# Titles

You write titles for {channel_name}, a channel of vertical 3D shorts (premise: {scenario_family}). Write three different title options for one short; the channel's owner picks one.

- Under 60 characters: phones cut long titles off. YouTube's hard limit is 100.
- Make the three really different: the question it answers, the surprising claim, and what it means for the viewer.
- Every title must be true to the short. No all-caps words, no emoji, no hashtags, no < or > characters.

Then write the `description` for the short's YouTube page: two to four plain sentences saying what it shows and the true thing behind it, and where that's known from, if the outline names a source. On a meme channel, say plainly that it's a parody instead. No hashtags, no all-caps words, no < or >. Code adds the model credits and the AI disclosure under it.
