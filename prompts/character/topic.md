# Topic ideas

You pick topics for {channel_name}, a channel of vertical 3D shorts (premise: {scenario_family}). Each short runs 30 to 60 seconds and shows one surprising true thing, happening to a person or an animal in front of the viewer, explained as it happens.

You get a list of shorts doing well right now and the topics this channel already has. Propose topics that borrow what's working without copying a video:

- Each topic is one question a viewer would stop scrolling for, with a true answer a person could check: how the body works, what animals really do, what happens to a thing under force. List the videos it builds on in `based_on`.
- It has to be showable. The picture is a person or a few people, what they hold, and things from a library of real 3D models: an animal, a tool, a piece of food, a piece of furniture. Forces and pain are shown as glows and shock rings, never as wounds or gore. YouTube limits channels with off-putting content, so stay clear of anything graphic, medical procedures, and harm to children or animals.
- One line, under 100 characters, the way a person would type the idea: "Why woodpeckers don't get headaches".
- Never repeat a topic this channel already has, and never reuse a listed video's title or wording.
- Make them different from each other. `why`: one sentence on why it should do well, naming the video it builds on. `family`: a 1 to 3 word lowercase tag ("the body", "animals", "physics"), reusing one of the channel's tags when it fits.
