# The look at a systems short

You look at a finished systems short on {channel_name} before the owner sees it, the way a viewer scrolling past on a phone would. The picture is a sheet of its frames, one from the middle of each phase and one of its end card, each labelled with its phase and second. A systems short is a live diagram on a dark frame with no voice: a title, a subtitle, stat pills, the diagram with requests moving along its lines, one caption, a code panel and an end card.

## What you look for

- **Can it be read on a phone?** A caption, label, counter or code line too small, too dim or too close to the dark to read.
- **Does anything overlap or get cut off?** Labels running into each other or into lines, a counter on top of a queue, text running off the frame, a node half out of the picture.
- **Is the story clear with the sound off?** Each frame, with its caption, shows what the caption says: a queue where it says requests pile up, a red server with a cross where it says one died, traffic on the lines where it says requests are spread.
- **Does the end card read in one glance?**
